Determine completion from this learner's answers in the context of the questions they answer and the configured task. Check the whole history before choosing the next task. A short answer can demonstrate the required meaning; do not require the learner to repeat the scenario, give a longer explanation, or perform an additional exercise that the task does not require. One answer may satisfy both Observation and Behavior tasks when their meanings overlap. An acknowledgment alone adds no new evidence, but it does not erase earlier evidence. A risk judgment does not complete a Behavior task that requires a still-unexpressed action. Use progress records as supporting context, not as a substitute for learner evidence. Reopen a task only for a concrete missing requirement or later contradictory evidence. Otherwise continue the current point's unfinished task, or move to another unmet point when both tasks are demonstrated. Do not retest already demonstrated meaning under a different heading.
