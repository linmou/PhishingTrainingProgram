Identify the first substantive instructional move in the response. Ignore brief acknowledgment of what the learner already supplied. Then compare that move with the declared instruction:

- protective_instruction passes when the first instructional move directly stops or redirects proposed imminent unsafe conduct before exploration. A conditional stated intention or advice to someone else still permits direct protection. A mere question that postpones protection does not realize it. Whether protection was required is checked separately.
- correction passes when the response explicitly revises the learner's false inference and supplies the connected safe action required by T01. Participation management is not instructional correction. Correcting an inference first and then giving safe advice remains correction; its protective benefit does not relabel it. Missing safe action fails.
- scaffolding passes when a useful focused hint, question or comparison cue leaves the targeted identification, relationship or inference to the learner. This includes a focused imperative comparison whose answer is not supplied. No question mark or special template is required. Supplying the conclusion, then asking a question tag or telling the learner to execute it, is not scaffolding.
- explanation passes when the response supplies permitted factual elaboration or teaches a usable method and its purpose. Before a focused scaffold fails, explanation may challenge a misconception. Do not force correction merely because a fact differs from the learner's belief. A focused cue leaving the targeted inference unresolved is scaffolding, not explanation; this specific distinction takes precedence over the general permission for procedural teaching. Execution of a completely taught method to retrieve an external result alone is not an unresolved learning inference.
