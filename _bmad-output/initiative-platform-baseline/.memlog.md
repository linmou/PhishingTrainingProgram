- (direction) Initiative is a baseline of built capabilities: epics list invariants as requirement lines, no stories for built work (stories only for new work). Source: owner, 2026-10-22.
- (requirement) R1 (FR-1, FR-2, FR-3, NFR-4): a person joins with a name and role, keeps that identity across reloads, and leaves; no account or password is needed.
- (requirement) R2 (FR-10, FR-11, FR-12, FR-13): a student finds rooms and enters them (with the room password if set); the first student becomes the room's only learner for the room's whole life; everyone else observes read-only.
- (requirement) R3 (FR-30, FR-52, NFR-1): only the room owner uses tutor controls, including turning Guard on or off; no AI text reaches a learner unless the owner sends it; the room owner is never the learner of their own room.
- (requirement) R4 (FR-4, FR-5, FR-6, FR-7, FR-8, FR-9): a tutor creates a room of one practice type, fixed at creation, from a template or a pinned scenario; test rooms stay out of teaching lists; the owner sees the rooms they own and can delete one after confirming.
- (requirement) R5 (FR-14, FR-15, FR-16, NFR-8): every participant sees the same live conversation within about 2 seconds, and the learner rates every tutor reply before writing again.
- (requirement) R6 (FR-18, FR-19, FR-20, FR-44, NFR-9): every learner has learner-owned learning targets; each learner message is judged against each target on the server, and the quality of that judging is governed by the AI behavior specifications.
- (requirement) R7 (FR-21, FR-22, FR-23, FR-53): learning progress moves only through the defined states and events, is shown to the owner, can be corrected by hand by the owner, and pauses while Guard is on.
- (requirement) R8 (FR-24 to FR-29, NFR-9, NFR-10): the owner requests an AI suggestion, reviews, edits and sends it, and sets the AI role; a change of model or prompt is re-evaluated before release.
- (requirement) R9 (FR-31 to FR-43, NFR-3): when a learner moves a target from Partial to Covered, a transfer assessment becomes due; the owner reviews and sends or rejects it; the learner answers with option controls; the server grades two attempts and saves result messages, publishing the correct answer only after the second wrong attempt.
- (requirement) R10 (FR-7, FR-44 to FR-49): in a cyberbullying room the learner writes about a pinned scenario, the server judges against five shared targets, and the owner replies through reviewed character drafts that every participant sees under the same character.
- (requirement) R11 (FR-50, FR-51): every participant can download the room's export, and the owner's reviews of AI drafts are recorded for later analysis.
- (requirement) R12 (NFR-2, NFR-5, NFR-6, NFR-7, NFR-11): grading and progress are decided on the server, repeating a request has no duplicate effect, state survives reloads and tabs, failures are visible, and an outdated client is told to reload.
- (requirement) R13 (NFR-12): the app is a responsive web app for desktop, tablet and phone in current mainstream browsers.
- (constraint) C1 to C6 of the product constitution bind every epic; AD-1 to AD-14 of the architecture spine bind every epic that touches shared state.
- (assumption) A tutor who is not the room owner takes part as an observer (PRD section 12).
- (decision) Epic split: 10 capability epics in dependency order, platform guarantees first. Source: slice rules (one capability, or a tightly coupled pair).
