# Rooms and templates

## Description

A tutor creates a room of one practice type, fixed at creation, from a template or a pinned scenario. Test rooms stay out of teaching lists. The owner sees the rooms they own and can delete one after confirming.

## Outcome

For anyone adding a practice type or changing room setup: the room lifecycle and its fixed practice type are written once.

## Boundaries

- Touch point: the learner seat and joining belong to epic-identity-and-access; the five cyberbullying targets belong to epic-cyberbullying-rooms; phishing learning-target setup belongs to epic-learning-progress.
- Not here: reuse of rooms or release of the learner seat.
