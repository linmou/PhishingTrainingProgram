# AI suggestions and tutor review

## Description

The room owner requests an AI suggestion for a learner message, reviews, edits and sends it, and sets the AI role. A change of AI model or prompt is re-evaluated before release.

## Outcome

For anyone changing AI drafts or the review flow: the input, output, audience and review rules are written once.

## Boundaries

- Touch point: assessment drafts replace the suggestion when an assessment is due (epic-transfer-assessment); character drafts belong to epic-cyberbullying-rooms; review records belong to epic-exports-and-records.
- Not here: the quality of AI output, which the AI behavior specifications govern.
