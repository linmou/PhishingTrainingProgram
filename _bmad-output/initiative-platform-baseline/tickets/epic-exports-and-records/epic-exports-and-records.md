# Exports and review records

## Description

Every participant can download the room's export as TXT or JSON, with content that depends on their role. The room owner's reviews of AI drafts are recorded for later analysis.

## Outcome

For anyone adding data to exports or records: what is exported, to whom, and what is recorded is written once.

## Boundaries

- Touch point: assessment attempts belong to epic-transfer-assessment; character replies belong to epic-cyberbullying-rooms; the review actions belong to epic-ai-suggestions.
- Not here: IP addresses or browser information, which are never recorded.
