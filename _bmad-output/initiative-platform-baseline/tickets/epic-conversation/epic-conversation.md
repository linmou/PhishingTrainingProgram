# Live conversation and ratings

## Description

Every participant sees the same live conversation within about 2 seconds, with typing indicators. The learner rates every tutor reply before writing again.

## Outcome

For anyone changing messaging or ratings: the shared-conversation and rating-gate rules are written once.

## Boundaries

- Touch point: the content of tutor replies belongs to epic-ai-suggestions and epic-cyberbullying-rooms; result messages belong to epic-transfer-assessment.
- Not here: judging of learner messages (epic-learning-progress).
